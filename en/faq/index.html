Правила та умови відпочинку

Часті запитання (FAQ)

Відповіді на найпоширеніші запитання щодо заселення в готелі «H2O» та «Колиба», роботи басейнів і SPA, бані на дровах, риболовлі та проведення банкетів.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Hotels H2O, Koliba, and the Cottage?

For Hotel H2O and Hotel Koliba rooms, check-in starts at 14:00 and check-out is by 12:00. For the two-story Lakeside Cottage (200 sqm), check-in is after 15:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

Are breakfast and Pool & SPA access included in the room rate?

For all Hotel H2O room categories, breakfast (09:00–11:00) and Pool & SPA access are included. For Hotel Koliba and the Lakeside Cottage, breakfast and SPA entry are paid separately.

What is the children's policy and extra bed pricing?

Children under 5 stay free without an extra bed. At Hotel H2O: baby crib (under 3 y.o.) is 150 UAH, extra bed for children 6–14 is 1,000 UAH, adults 1,400 UAH. At Hotel Koliba: extra bed for children 6–14 is 400 UAH, adults 600 UAH.

What are the opening hours of the H2O pools and SPA, and how do they operate in winter?

Pool & SPA hours: Mon–Fri 16:00–21:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–21:00. Facilities include a Finnish sauna, Turkish hammam, and jacuzzi. In winter, the outdoor pool is open exclusively for cold contrast plunge after the sauna.

How much do the wood-fired banya, herbal chan, and fishing cost?

Wood-fired banya is 800 UAH/hr (min 2 hrs), banya + herbal chan is 1,400 UAH/hr (min 2 hrs, up to 5 guests). Promotions: Mon–Thu 4th hour free, Fri–Sun 5th hour free. Lake fishing: day 700 UAH, night 1,000 UAH, 24h 1,200 UAH.

What is the capacity of the banquet hall for weddings or corporate events?

The Grand Banquet Hall accommodates up to 150 guests. For intimate celebrations we offer the Red Hall and waterfront summer terrace, backed by 17 years of event hospitality (+380 96 030 78 88).
